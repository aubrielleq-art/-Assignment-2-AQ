# Sheridan College
  
I went to Sheridan College from around the years of 2021-2025. My first program was Art Fundamentals. Growing up, I've always been very artistic and crafty, so I thought doing art in college was what I would've wanted. I was self taught, so I believed learning the fundamentals in a formal setting would make my foundations stronger.
  
  It started out great. And for a while, it did.  But somewhere along the lines, I grew to really dislike it. Art school felt too restrictive for me, and I found myself losing a lot of motivation and the will to create. Though I did understand the reasoning for how the lessons were structured, I just wasn't enjoying myself.
  
  So then I ended up dropping the program, and switching to Marketing in the fall. I knew I also liked business somewhat, and Marketing was still a way for me to explore my creativity. With all its ups and downs, I ended up graduating from the Marketing program in 2025. Despite all the challenges I faced, overall I rate it an A.
