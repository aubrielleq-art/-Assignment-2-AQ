# Marketing Co op
  
My second co-op placement happened to be at Sheridan's own social media department.  I worked along with another student, filming and creating all sorts of content for Sheridan's official social media pages. We would promote school events, and travel a bit to Sheridan's other campuses as well, such as the Hazel McCallion campus, Trafalgar campus, and Davis campus. It was a wonderful experience getting to see all the different campuses Sheridan had offer. 

Rating: A
