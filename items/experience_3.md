# Swimming 

While I did Taekwondo, I also did a lot of swimming. I first started swimming when I was about 8yrs old. When I was about 20, I wanted to try doing the lifeguard course.  In the lifeguard course, there were two main components, the written first aid exam, and the physical exam.  Learning first aid wasn't too challenging for me, however I struggled quite a bit with the physical exam. Specifically, the time swim challenge. The challenge was to do a continuous swim of 400m within 12 minutes. I think back then I managed to get it done within about 11 minutes. But in order to get Bronze Medal, ideally it should be done within 10 minutes. Eventually, I decided not to go through with more training because I felt that if I had to act under a life-saving situation, it'd be too stressful for me.   

Rating: B
